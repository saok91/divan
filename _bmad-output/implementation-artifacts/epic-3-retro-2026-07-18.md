### Delivery narrative

## What went well

- **Sequencing rule held:** Master-data / invariants / read fidelity only; product Start/Return/Terminate/Transfer/comp mutations deferred to Epic 4. No throwaway CRUD-as-SoT.
- **Projection-writer idiom matured:** `apply*Projection` / `close*Projection` + half-open intervals + PG `EXCLUDE`/`btree_gist` established in 3.2 and reused for compensation (3.4), Primary (3.7), Secondary (3.8).
- **Synced dual + dependency hooks:** OrgUnit `active` + interval (3.5) became the pattern for job refs (3.6) and Assignment deactivate gates.
- **Fail-closed AR stubs:** Dictionary dispositions, compensation `later`, Secondary inventory, management Role registry — shipped without inventing AR-DATA / AR-SECONDARY / AR-ACCESS / AR-TEMPORAL production policy.
- **Derived management without shadow truth (3.9):** Decision B keeps derive and access adapters fail-closed on ambiguity (`AMBIGUOUS_*`); Secondary never consulted for reporting edges.
- **Module seam proven (3.4):** Real `compensation` schema + repo ownership replaced the typed seam; AD-3 / NFR-8 isolation without payroll.
- **Carry-in intelligence:** Later stories (especially 3.7–3.9) encoded prior review anti-patterns (FORBIDDEN misuse, seed shape, race-safe close, active-as-of hooks, BOM hygiene).
- **Epic 2 prep largely applied:** 3↔4 rule in 3.1 Gate; PopulationPort cutover plan named; AR numbers fail-closed; envelope/category discipline continued.

## Challenges

- **PopulationPort still fixture-backed by default:** Adapter + Person UUID target exist (3.1→3.9), but default registry remains fixture — “complete” population authz for real workforce not finished.
- **AR-TEMPORAL gaps on writers:** `assertRefsExist` active-as-of and hook deactivate-as-of deferred identically on Primary (3.7) and Secondary (3.8); apply may attach inactive refs until policy lands.
- **Transfer coverage sequencing:** Coverage assert-only on Primary writers — Epic 4.7 Transfer must assert coverage **after** atomic close+open; enforcing on mid-Transfer `close` alone breaks the flow.
- **Thin shared-DB selfchecks:** Pattern across 3.3/3.4/3.9 (+ lineage); mutate seed DB with best-effort restore; not Vitest/CI isolated — risk rises when Epic 4 writers amplify.
- **Seed quality bar rose mid-epic:** Early seeds accepted weak shape (length≥N); later reviews demanded FK shape, exact cardinality, `$transaction` — uneven early vs late debt.
- **Error semantics drift:** Domain/config misses mapped as FORBIDDEN in early stories; BAD_REQUEST vs FORBIDDEN discipline improved but had to be re-taught via review.
- **sprint-status encoding hygiene:** UTF-8 BOM / mojibake introduced or reintroduced when editing status (3.7–3.9) — process, not domain, tax.
- **Process Status alignment action still formally open:** Epic 3 story files and sprint-status keys **are** aligned at `done`, but Epic 1 #8 / Epic 2 #2 action items were never closed in tracking.

## Key insights

1. **Constraint stories without UI are a first-class delivery shape** — Employment / Primary / Secondary / derive shipped integrity without product mutation surfaces; AC-allowed no-UI was a velocity and sequencing win.
2. **EXCLUDE + race-safe `updateMany` is the temporal write contract** — check-then-act alone is insufficient; map `23P01` and reject empty/whitespace ends.
3. **Hooks that only match `endDateExclusive: null` are wrong** — active-as-of must include finite-end still-active rows; deactivate paths must ensure hooks are registered.
4. **Fail-closed ambiguity must be shared across derive and access** — silent skip for reporting while access fails closed is a product bug class; 3.9 closed it for management edges.
5. **Epic 4 risk is writer atomicity + AR-TEMPORAL disposition, not missing entities** — FR5–13 spine is in place; event flows must respect Transfer coverage order and inactive-ref policy.
